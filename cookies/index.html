Cookie Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

telev.app does not use cookies.

The Website doesn't set cookies or use similar technologies such as local storage, tracking pixels, or device fingerprinting. It has no analytics, advertising, or social media plugins, and it loads no third-party scripts or fonts. That's why you don't see a cookie banner: there is nothing to consent to under the EU ePrivacy Directive, the UK PECR, or similar laws.

Our host, GitHub Pages, processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and protect the service. This is described in our Privacy Policy.

Links on the Website to other sites, such as Google Play or Telegram, take you to services with their own cookie policies.

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookie.
