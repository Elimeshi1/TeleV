Delete Your Data

Last updated: October 3, 2026

TeleV doesn't have its own accounts. You sign in with your existing Telegram account, and we keep no copy of your data on any server. Here's how to remove everything TeleV stores.

1. Delete the data on your TV

  1. Open TeleV and go to Profile → Log out. This ends the session and erases the sign-in keys, cache, and preferences from the TV.
  2. To remove everything, uninstall TeleV from the TV (Settings → Apps → TeleV → Uninstall).

2. Disconnect the TV remotely

If you no longer have access to the TV, open Telegram on your phone or computer, go to Settings → Devices, select the TeleV session, and choose Terminate session. TeleV loses access immediately.

3. Data held by Telegram

Your messages, channels, and account are stored by Telegram, not by TeleV. To delete them, use Telegram's own tools, or delete your Telegram account at my.telegram.org. Note that deleting your Telegram account deletes it for all Telegram apps.

4. Data we hold

The only personal data we may hold is email you've sent us. To have it deleted, write to support@telev.app from the same address. We'll confirm deletion within 30 days.

More details are in our Privacy Policy.
