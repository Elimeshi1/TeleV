Data Deletion

Last updated: October 3, 2026

TeleV does not maintain user accounts or store user data on its servers. Data stored by the App can be deleted as follows:

  1. In the App, select Profile → Log out, or uninstall the App. All locally stored data is deleted.
  2. To revoke access remotely, open Telegram, select Settings → Devices, and terminate the TeleV session.

Data held by Telegram is subject to Telegram's policies and can be managed through Telegram. Requests concerning correspondence held by us may be sent to support@telev.app.
