Which TVs and devices can run Telegram?

Telegram has no official app for TVs. TeleV is a free Telegram app for Android TV and Google TV, so it runs on any TV or streaming device that has the Google Play Store and Android 8.0 or newer.

How to install

On the TV, open the Google Play Store, search for TeleV and install it. You can also install it from Google Play on the web by choosing your TV from the device list. Then follow the step-by-step setup guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is there an official Telegram app for Android TV?

No. Telegram doesn't offer an official app for Android TV or Google TV. TeleV is an independent, unofficial app that brings Telegram videos and live streams to the TV.

Can I watch Telegram on a Samsung or LG TV?

Not directly, because Samsung and LG TVs can't run Android apps. Connect a Chromecast with Google TV or a Google TV Streamer to the TV and install TeleV on it.

Does TeleV work on Amazon Fire TV?

No. TeleV is distributed through Google Play, which isn't available on Fire TV devices.

Does TeleV work on Chromecast with Google TV?

Yes. Chromecast with Google TV and Google TV Streamer are fully supported.

What Android version does TeleV need?

Android 8.0 or newer, on an Android TV or Google TV device.
