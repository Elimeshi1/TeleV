Accessibility Statement

Last updated: October 3, 2026

We want everyone to be able to use the TeleV website and app, including people with disabilities.

The website

The website telev.app was built to meet the Israeli Standard IS 5568 and the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. Among other things, it provides:

  • Full keyboard navigation with a visible focus indicator, and a "skip to main content" link.
  • Semantic HTML with a correct heading structure, page language, and text direction (right-to-left in Hebrew).
  • Text alternatives for all meaningful images.
  • High color contrast between text and background.
  • A responsive layout that works when zoomed up to 200% and on mobile screens.
  • Respect for the "reduce motion" setting in your operating system.

The site was checked with the axe automated accessibility checker and with keyboard-only navigation.

The app

The TeleV app is fully operable with a TV remote's arrow keys and OK button, highlights the focused item clearly, uses large readable text, and supports English and Hebrew (right-to-left).

Known limitations

Screenshots of the app are illustrations and are described in their alternative text. Video content shown in the app comes from Telegram channels, and whether captions are available depends on the channel that published it.

Contact the accessibility coordinator

If you run into an accessibility problem or need information in an accessible format, please contact us. We will respond as soon as possible and within 14 days.
