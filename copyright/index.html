Copyright & Content Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

TeleV doesn't host content

TeleV is a viewer for Telegram. Every video, live stream, image, and message shown in the App is stored and served by Telegram and comes from the user's own Telegram account or from public Telegram channels. We don't upload, store, index, recommend, or curate content. The App ships with no channel lists, and we can't access, remove, or block content on Telegram.

Reporting infringing or illegal content

If content on Telegram infringes your copyright or is illegal, report it to Telegram, which hosts it and can remove it:

  • Copyright notices: dmca@telegram.org.
  • Illegal content (including under the EU Digital Services Act): use the "Report" option on the channel or message in any official Telegram app, or Telegram's channels described at telegram.org/faq.

Your responsibility as a user

You're responsible for the channels you follow and watch. Please respect creators' rights and the law where you live. See section 3 of our Terms of Use.

Our own content

The TeleV name, logo, app, and website are ours. If you believe something we published (the App's own interface or this Website, not Telegram content) infringes your rights, email support@telev.app with:

  • the work you believe is infringed;
  • where it appears in TeleV or on telev.app;
  • your contact details;
  • a statement that you believe in good faith that the use isn't authorized, and that the information in your notice is accurate.

We'll review it promptly and remove infringing material.
