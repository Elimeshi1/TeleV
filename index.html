Everything you follow on Telegram, on the big screen

No casting, no phone in hand. A real TV app for the channels you already watch.

Sign in from the couch

Scan a QR code with Telegram on your phone, or use your phone number. Two-step verification is supported.

Live streams

Watch channel and group live broadcasts in real time, and flip between active streams with the remote.

Smooth video playback

Videos stream straight from Telegram with smart caching, so even very large files play without filling your TV's storage.

Continue watching

Pick up any video right where you left off. Adjust playback speed when you want to go faster.

Favorite channels

Hold OK on a channel to pin it as a favorite, so it always shows first on your home screen.

Search & archive

Find public channels and browse their video history. Archived chats get a screen of their own.

Made for the remote

Every screen is designed for D-pad navigation and readable from across the room.

Up and running in a minute

Install TeleV

Find TeleV in the Google Play Store on your TV, or install it from Google Play on the web.

Scan the QR code

On your phone, open Telegram → Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device (Log in on Computer), and scan the code on the TV.

Start watching

Your channels and chats appear as rows of videos. Pick one with the remote and press OK.

Frequently asked questions

Can I watch Telegram on my TV?

Yes. Install TeleV from Google Play on your Android TV or Google TV device, scan the QR code with the Telegram app on your phone, and the videos from your channels and chats appear on the TV.

Is TeleV free?

Yes, TeleV is free to download and use.

Which devices does TeleV support?

Any Android TV or Google TV device running Android 8.0 or newer: smart TVs from Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips and others, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, Xiaomi Mi Box, Nvidia Shield and most Android TV boxes.

Can I watch Telegram live streams on TV?

Yes. TeleV plays live streams and video chats broadcast in channels and groups in real time, and you can switch between active live streams with the remote.

Is my Telegram account safe?

TeleV is built on TDLib, Telegram's official library. Your data goes directly between your TV and Telegram's servers. The developer runs no servers and collects no personal data. See the privacy policy.

Can I send messages from TeleV?

No. TeleV is a viewer built for watching. It doesn't send messages and never uses the microphone or camera.

Does TeleV support Hebrew?

Yes. TeleV is fully available in English and Hebrew, including a right-to-left layout.
