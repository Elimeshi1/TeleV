Up and running in a minute

Install TeleV

Find TeleV in the Google Play Store on your TV, or install it from Google Play on the web.

Scan the QR code

On your phone, open Telegram → Settings → Devices → Link Desktop Device (Log in on Computer), and scan the code on the TV.

Start watching

Your channels and chats appear as rows of videos. Pick one with the remote and press OK.

Frequently asked questions

What is TeleV?

TeleV is a free Telegram app for Android TV and Google TV. It shows the videos and live streams from your Telegram channels, groups and chats on your TV, with full remote control navigation.

Is there an official Telegram app for Android TV?

No. Telegram doesn't offer an official app for Android TV or Google TV. TeleV is an independent, unofficial app built on TDLib, Telegram's official open-source library.

How do I watch Telegram on my TV?

Install TeleV from Google Play on your Android TV or Google TV, then scan the QR code on the screen with the Telegram app on your phone. Your channels and chats appear on the TV. See the step-by-step guide.

Is TeleV free?

Yes, TeleV is free to download and use.

Which devices does TeleV support?

Any Android TV or Google TV device running Android 8.0 or newer, including Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, Nvidia Shield, Xiaomi Mi Box, and Sony, TCL, Hisense and Philips smart TVs. See the full list of supported devices.

Can I watch Telegram on a Samsung or LG TV?

Not directly, because Samsung and LG TVs can't run Android apps. Connect a Chromecast with Google TV or a Google TV Streamer and install TeleV on it.

Can I watch Telegram live streams on TV?

Yes. TeleV plays live streams and video chats broadcast in channels and groups in real time, and you can switch between active live streams with the remote. How to watch live streams.

Is my Telegram account safe?

TeleV is built on TDLib, Telegram's official library. Your data goes directly between your TV and Telegram's servers, and TeleV doesn't collect personal data. See the privacy policy.

Can I send messages from TeleV?

No. TeleV is built for watching. It doesn't send messages and never uses the microphone or camera.

What languages does TeleV support?

TeleV is currently available in English and Hebrew. More languages are on the way.
