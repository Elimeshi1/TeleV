Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is handled when you use TeleV, the Android TV app (the "App"), and the website telev.app (the "Website"). The App and Website are provided by Eli Meshi, an independent developer ("we", "us"). You can reach us at elimz2000@gmail.com.

In short: we don't run any servers that receive your data. TeleV connects your TV directly to Telegram. We don't collect, sell, or share your personal information, we don't use analytics or advertising, and the Website sets no cookies.

1. TeleV is an unofficial Telegram app

TeleV is an independent third-party client built on Telegram's official open-source library (TDLib) and the Telegram API. It is not made, endorsed, or operated by Telegram. When you use TeleV, you use your own Telegram account, and Telegram processes your data under its own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Telegram is an independent controller of that data. We have no access to it.

2. Information the App handles

2.1 Sent directly to Telegram, never to us

  • Sign-in details: your phone number, the login code, and your two-step verification password (if you sign in by phone), or the QR login token (if you sign in by QR code).
  • Technical details Telegram needs to run a session: device type ("Android TV"), app version, system language, and your IP address, which every internet connection reveals.
  • Requests for content: the chats, channels, videos, and live streams you open, and the searches you run.

All of this travels encrypted between your TV and Telegram's servers. It does not pass through any server operated by us.

2.2 Stored only on your TV

  • Your Telegram session keys, so you stay signed in.
  • A temporary cache of video files and thumbnails, kept small and cleared automatically.
  • Your preferences: language, favorite channels, and the playback position of videos you haven't finished ("Continue watching").

This data never leaves your device, isn't backed up to the cloud (the App disables Android backup), and is deleted when you log out, clear the App's data, or uninstall it.

2.3 What we never do

  • We don't receive your messages, contacts, phone number, viewing history, or any other account data.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • The App can't send messages, and it never accesses your microphone, camera, location, or contacts. These permissions are removed from the App entirely.
  • We don't sell or "share" personal information (as those terms are defined in the California Consumer Privacy Act), and we don't use it for targeted advertising or profiling.

3. Third-party services involved

  • Telegram provides the messaging service, accounts, and all content shown in the App. Telegram Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play distributes the App. Google may collect information about downloads, and, if you enabled it on your device, crash and performance diagnostics, which it shares with developers only in aggregated or anonymized form. Google Privacy Policy.
  • GitHub (Microsoft) hosts the Website and a small settings file the App checks on launch to see whether an update is required. Like any web host, GitHub sees the IP address and basic technical details of the request and keeps short-lived security logs. GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. The Website

The Website is a static site. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no third-party trackers, fonts, or scripts, so no cookie banner is needed. Our host, GitHub Pages, processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and protect the service, as described above.

5. If you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to reply and to keep a record of the conversation, and we delete it when it is no longer needed, generally within 24 months. Our email is provided by Google (Gmail).

6. Legal bases (EEA, UK, and similar laws)

Where laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) apply, the limited processing we perform relies on:

  • Performance of a contract: running the App's features you ask for, on your own device.
  • Legitimate interests: answering your emails, delivering and securing the Website, and checking whether an App update is required.
  • Legal obligations, where they apply.

7. Retention

On-device data stays until you log out, clear the App's data, or uninstall the App. Emails are kept as described in section 5. Third parties keep data according to their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, to withdraw consent, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. This includes rights under the GDPR (EU/EEA), the UK GDPR, the Swiss FADP, the Israeli Privacy Protection Law, US state laws (such as California's CCPA/CPRA, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, and others), Brazil's LGPD, Canada's PIPEDA, Australia's Privacy Act, Japan's APPI, South Korea's PIPA, India's DPDP Act, and similar laws.

Because we don't hold your App data, most requests are fulfilled directly (step-by-step instructions are on the Delete Your Data page):

  • Delete on-device data: in the App go to Profile → Log out, or uninstall the App.
  • End the TV session remotely: in any Telegram app, go to Settings → Devices and terminate the TeleV session.
  • Data held by Telegram: use Telegram's own tools, or contact Telegram as described in its Privacy Policy.
  • Data we hold (emails): write to elimz2000@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days (or sooner if your local law requires) and may need to verify your request. You may use an authorized agent where your law allows.

You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority, for example a supervisory authority in the EU, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or the Israel Privacy Protection Authority. We'd appreciate the chance to address your concern first.

We honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals. Since we don't sell or share personal information, there is nothing further to opt out of.

9. International transfers

We are based in Israel, a country recognized by the European Commission as providing adequate data protection. Telegram, Google, and GitHub may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards, such as the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

10. Security

The App uses Telegram's official library with encrypted connections, stores its session in the App's private storage on your device, and has backups disabled. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Keep your TV and Telegram account protected, for example with two-step verification.

11. Children

TeleV requires a Telegram account, so you must meet Telegram's minimum age requirements to use it. TeleV is not directed at children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by the law where you live, such as 16 in some EU countries), and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date, and for significant changes we will also give notice in the App or on the Website.
