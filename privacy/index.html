Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how personal information is processed in connection with the TeleV application for Android TV, Google TV, and Android phones and tablets (the "App") and the website telev.app (the "Website"), provided by the TeleV developer ("we", "us"). Contact: support@telev.app.

1. Relationship with Telegram

The App is an unofficial third-party client for Telegram and is not affiliated with Telegram. The App connects the user's device directly to Telegram's servers. Information exchanged with Telegram is processed by Telegram as an independent controller under its Privacy Policy. We do not have access to that information.

2. Information processed by the App

The following information is transmitted directly between the device and Telegram and is not received by us:

  • sign-in information (phone number, verification code, two-step verification password, or QR login token);
  • technical information required to establish a session, such as device type, app version, language, and IP address;
  • requests for chats, channels, and media opened by the user.

The following information is stored locally on the device only: session credentials, a temporary media cache, videos the user downloads for offline viewing, proxy settings, and user preferences. Session credentials, the media cache, and downloaded videos are deleted upon logout; all of this information is deleted upon uninstallation of the App.

If the user configures a proxy, the App's connection to Telegram passes through the proxy server chosen by the user. That server is operated by a third party, not by us.

Continue on TV

On a phone or tablet, the "Continue on TV" feature sends a video or live stream to the App on a TV on the same local network. The TV announces itself on the local network with its device name and the identifier of the Telegram account it is signed in to, so that the phone lists only TVs signed in to the same account. When the user selects a TV, the phone sends it, over the local network only, the identifiers of the chat and the message or live stream and the playback position. The TV then opens the video through its own connection to Telegram. This information does not pass through our servers.

We do not sell or share personal information, and the App does not contain advertising components.

Crash and diagnostic reports

To find and fix problems, the App sends crash and diagnostic reports to Firebase Crashlytics, a service provided by Google. A report is sent when the App crashes or stops responding, or when it fails at a task the user sees (for example, a live stream that cannot be played or a connection to Telegram that cannot be established). A report contains:

  • technical information about the failure, such as the error and a technical log of the App's recent internal activity;
  • device information, such as the device model, Android version, memory, processor type, language, and app version;
  • a random support code generated by the App, a Firebase installation identifier, and the IP address from which the report is sent.

Reports do not contain your phone number, Telegram account details, messages, chat or channel names, or search text. The support code is shown on the App's settings screen and is not linked to your Telegram account; you may quote it when contacting us so that we can find the reports from your device. Reports are processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and retained for up to 90 days.

Usage statistics

To understand which features are used and where the App is slow or fails, the App sends usage statistics to Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. These statistics contain:

  • which screens are opened and which features are used, such as playing a video or live stream, adding a favorite, downloading a video, sending a video to a TV, searching, or changing a setting;
  • measurements of playback and loading, such as how long a video was watched, how long it took to start, and whether it paused to buffer or failed, expressed as durations, counts, and categories;
  • the steps of signing in, without the phone number or code entered, and general error codes;
  • device information, such as the device type and model, Android version, memory class, language, and app version, together with a random app-instance identifier and the approximate location (country and region) that Google derives from the IP address.

Usage statistics do not contain your phone number, Telegram account details, messages, chat or channel names, video titles, or search text. The App does not collect the advertising ID, does not use these statistics for advertising, and has advertising personalization disabled. Statistics are processed by Google under the Firebase privacy terms and retained for up to 14 months.

3. Website

The Website does not use cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies. Our hosting provider processes technical information, such as IP addresses, as necessary to deliver the Website.

4. Correspondence

If you contact us, we process your email address and the content of your message for the purpose of responding. Correspondence is retained for up to 24 months.

5. Service providers

Limited technical information may be processed by our service providers, including application distribution (Google Play), crash and diagnostic reporting and usage statistics (Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase, by Google), website hosting, domain, and email service providers, solely to provide their services.

6. Legal basis

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, processing is based on the performance of a contract (providing the App's functionality) and our legitimate interests (operating the Website, diagnosing and fixing faults in the App, understanding how the App is used in order to improve it, and responding to inquiries).

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to access, rectify, or erase your personal information, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. Information stored by the App can be deleted directly, as described on the Data Deletion page. Requests concerning information held by us may be sent to support@telev.app and will be answered within 30 days. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

8. International transfers

Information may be processed outside your country of residence by Telegram and our service providers, subject to appropriate safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. Security

Communication between the App and Telegram, and diagnostic reports and usage statistics sent to Firebase, are encrypted in transit. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

10. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, or under the minimum age required in their country. Use of the App requires a Telegram account and is subject to Telegram's age requirements.

11. Changes

We may amend this Privacy Policy. The current version will be published on this page with its effective date.
